I am a solicitor who helps individuals secure their status in the UK, reunite with loved ones, and challenge unfair or unlawful Home Office decisions.

I have over a decade of experience in family migration, human-rights-based applications, asylum and protection, domestic abuse, and citizenship.

My career began in the legal aid sector, advising children displaced by the conflicts in Syria and Afghanistan and helping them reunite safely with family in the UK.

I later worked on high-profile judicial review challenges to government practice and policy. This experience has shaped a compassionate, client-focused approach and an ability to identify arguments and routes others may overlook.

I provide legal services as a consultant solicitor, through Lawhive, an SRA-regulated law firm.

I can assist with the full spectrum of UK immigration work, including:

  • Family & Partner Visas (Appendix FM), including complex, 'exceptional circumstances' applications based on Article 8 ECHR — from £1,149.
  • British Citizenship, including naturalisation, registration of children and discretionary applications — from £599.
  • Applications for Indefinite Leave to Remain ('ILR') for people who wish to settle permanently in the UK — from £949.
  • Adult Dependent Relative visas (Appendix ADR), for people who need to be cared for by their family members in the UK — from £1,349.
  • Student and Visitor Visas for people wishing to study in, or visit the UK — from £349.
  • Private life applications (Appendix PL), for children, young adults and long-term residents who have built their lives in the UK — from £799.
  • Refugee Family Reunion, including under Appendix Child Relative (Sponsors with Protection) — from £799.
  • Asylum and Humanitarian Protection, including fresh claims (further submissions), appeals and settlement applications — from £699.
  • Domestic Abuse (MVDAC and Appendix VDA), for individuals needing temporary leave (from £149) or settlement (from £799) after experiencing domestic abuse.
  • Challenges to Home Office decisions, including administrative reviews, appeals and judicial review.
